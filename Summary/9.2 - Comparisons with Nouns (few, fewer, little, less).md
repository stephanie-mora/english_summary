
# Comparisons with Nouns (few, fewer, little, less)

We use **few** and **little** to talk about small quantities, and their comparative and superlative forms to compare quantities.

| Type | Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Countable (plural)** | few | fewer | the fewest |
| **Uncountable** | little | less | the least |

## Countable vs. Uncountable

| Type | Words | Use |
|------|-------|-----|
| **Countable (plural)** | few, fewer, the fewest | With plural nouns (friends, jobs, books) |
| **Uncountable** | little, less, the least | With uncountable nouns (work, money, time) |

### Few / Fewer / The Fewest → Countable Plural Nouns

- **Few** = not many (negative opinion)
- **A few** = some (positive opinion)
- **Fewer** = a smaller number (comparative)
- **The fewest** = the smallest number (superlative)

**Examples:**

- Nowadays there are **fewer jobs** for young people than ten years ago.
- She has **few friends** in the city. (negative — almost none)
- She has **a few friends** in the city. (positive — some)
- He made **the fewest mistakes** of all.

### Little / Less / The Least → Uncountable Nouns

- **Little** = not much (negative opinion)
- **A little** = some (positive opinion)
- **Less** = a smaller amount (comparative)
- **The least** = the smallest amount (superlative)

**Examples:**

- I have **less work** than last week.
- There is **little milk** in the fridge. (negative — almost none)
- There is **a little milk** in the fridge. (positive — some)
- She has **the least patience** of the three sisters.

## Fewer vs. Less: The Rule

| Use **fewer** with... | Use **less** with... |
|-----------------------|----------------------|
| Plural countable nouns | Uncountable nouns |
| fewer jobs | less work |
| fewer friends | less money |
| fewer cars | less time |
| fewer books | less information |

**Examples**

- **Fewer** people write letters these days because they have **less** time than they used to.
- When you can write an email in **less** than five minutes, why bother writing a letter?
- I think I've received **fewer** than ten letters in my entire life!

## Less of / Fewer of

Use **less of** and **fewer of** before pronouns and determiners.

**Examples:**

- I try to spend **less of** my time on the computer.
- **Fewer of** us are going this year.

## Less / Fewer Without a Noun

Use **less/fewer** without a noun when the meaning is clear.

**Examples:**

- Some people write letters, but **fewer** than before.
- I have **less** than I thought.

## Exceptions: Measurements and Numbers

Sometimes with **measurements and numbers** we use **less** even though the nouns are countable.

- Use **less** with numerals (1, 2, 3...) or when individual items are seen as a whole unit.
  - A text message usually has **less than** 160 characters.
  - You can write an email in **less than** five minutes.
- Use **fewer** to stress individual items.
  - This year I received **fewer than** ten letters.

## Informal Speech

Although considered grammatically incorrect, many native speakers use **less** with both countable and uncountable nouns in informal speech.

- *People write **less** letters than they used to.* (informal — grammatically: *fewer letters*)
- *This year I got **less** than ten letters.* (informal — grammatically: *fewer than ten letters*)

> **Note:** In formal writing, always use **fewer** with countable nouns and **less** with uncountable nouns.

## Graded Quantifiers

| Quantifier | Comparative | Superlative |
|------------|-------------|-------------|
| **many** (C) | more | most |
| **much** (U) | more | most |
| **few** (C) | fewer | fewest |
| **little** (U) | less | least |

## Comparison Table

| Countable | Uncountable |
|-----------|-------------|
| few | little |
| a few | a little |
| fewer | less |
| the fewest | the least |

## Common Mistakes

| ❌ Incorrect | ✅ Correct |
|--------------|------------|
| *less jobs* | *fewer jobs* |
| *fewer money* | *less money* |
| *less friends* | *fewer friends* |
| *fewer work* | *less work* |
| *less people* | *fewer people* (formal) |

> **Remember:**
> - **Fewer** → things you can count (jobs, friends, cars)
> - **Less** → things you cannot count (money, time, work)

## Key Points to Remember

1. Use **few / fewer / the fewest** with **countable plural nouns**.
2. Use **little / less / the least** with **uncountable nouns**.
3. **Fewer** is the comparative of **few**.
4. **Less** is the comparative of **little**.
5. Use **less of / fewer of** before pronouns and determiners.
6. In measurements and numbers, **less** is often used even with countable nouns.
7. In informal speech, **less** is often used with countable nouns, but this is grammatically incorrect.
8. Don't confuse **fewer** and **less** — it's a very common mistake!
