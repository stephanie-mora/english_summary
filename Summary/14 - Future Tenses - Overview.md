
# Future Tenses: Overview

In English, there are **many ways** to talk about the future. It's important to remember that we are expressing more than simply the time of the action. The future form we choose also expresses our **attitude** toward the future event.

> **Key idea:** All "future" tenses refer to a time **later than now**, but they also express our perspective on the event.

## The Four Future Tenses

| Tense | Structure | Example | Use |
|-------|-----------|---------|-----|
| **Simple Future** | will + base verb | I **will go**. | An action that will occur in the future |
| **Future Progressive** | will be + verb-ing | I **will be going**. | An ongoing action in the future |
| **Future Perfect** | will have + past participle | I **will have gone**. | An action completed before a specific future time |
| **Future Perfect Progressive** | will have been + verb-ing | I **will have been going**. | An ongoing action completed at a specific future time |

## 1. Simple Future Tense

> **Structure:** **Subject + will + base verb + complement**

| Type | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Affirmative** | S + will + V | I **will play** after breakfast. |
| **Negative** | S + will not (won't) + V | Susan **will not go** to Germany. |
| **Yes/No Question** | Will + S + V? | **Will** you **come** to the party? |
| **Wh- Question** | Wh- + will + S + V? | **Where** will you **go**? |

### Uses

- **Instant/spontaneous decisions** — *I'm hungry. I think I**'ll make** a sandwich.*
- **Future predictions based on belief** — *I'm sure you**'ll pass** the test.*
- **Promises** — *I **won't tell** anyone your secret.*
- **Offers** — *I**'ll carry** your bags for you.*
- **Requests** — ***Will** you **tell** Henry I called?*
- **Threats** — *If you do that again, I**'ll tell** Mum.*
- **Future facts** — *I**'ll be** back later tonight.*

### Shall

We can use **shall** instead of **will** for future time references with **I** and **we**. It is slightly more formal.

- We **shall** never forget this beautiful day.

It is also common to use **shall** in questions to make offers, suggestions, or ask for advice.

- **Shall I** carry these bags for you?
- **Shall I** open the window?
- What **shall I** tell Mary about the broken vase?

## 2. Future Progressive Tense

> **Structure:** **Subject + will be + verb-ing + complement**

| Type | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Affirmative** | S + will be + V-ing | I **will be playing** for an hour. |
| **Negative** | S + will not be + V-ing | I **won't be going** back. |
| **Yes/No Question** | Will + S + be + V-ing? | **Will** you **be joining** us? |
| **Wh- Question** | Wh- + will + S + be + V-ing? | **What** will you **be doing**? |

### Uses

- **An action in progress at a specific time in the future** — *This time tomorrow, I**'ll be flying** to Barbados.*
- **An action we see as new or temporary** — *I**'ll be working** for my Dad until I find a new job.*
- **Predictions or guesses about future events** — *He**'ll be coming** to the party, I guess.*
- **Polite enquiries** — ***Will** you **be joining** us for dinner?*

> **Note:** Stative verbs (know, want, believe, etc.) cannot be used in the future continuous. Use the **simple future** instead.

## 3. Future Perfect Tense

> **Structure:** **Subject + will have + past participle + complement**

| Type | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Affirmative** | S + will have + V3 | I **will have played** by breakfast. |
| **Negative** | S + will not have + V3 | I **won't have finished** by then. |
| **Yes/No Question** | Will + S + have + V3? | **Will** you **have graduated** by next year? |
| **Wh- Question** | Wh- + will + S + have + V3? | **What** will we **have submitted** by tonight? |

### Uses

- **An action that will be completed before a specific time in the future** — *Next September, we**'ll have been** married for 50 years.*
- **Use *by* or *by the time* to mean some time before** — *I**'ll have finished** this report **by the time** you're home.*
- **Use *in* to mean at the end of this period** — ***In** three years' time, I**'ll have completed** my degree.*

## 4. Future Perfect Progressive Tense

> **Structure:** **Subject + will have been + verb-ing + complement**

| Type | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Affirmative** | S + will have been + V-ing | I **will have been playing** for 2 hours. |
| **Negative** | S + will not have been + V-ing | I **won't have been waiting** long. |
| **Yes/No Question** | Will + S + have been + V-ing? | **Will** they **have been building** this house? |
| **Wh- Question** | Wh- + will + S + have been + V-ing? | **How long** will you **have been working** there? |

### Uses

- **To show that something will continue up until a particular event in the future** — *In October, I**'ll have been working** here for ten years.*
- **To show something finished just before another time action (cause and effect)** — *When I arrive, I**'ll have been working** all day, so I**'ll be** tired.*
- **With time expressions (by + then/tomorrow/next year, by the time, when)** — ***By the time** we arrive, we**'ll have been travelling** for fifteen hours.*

## Other Ways to Talk About the Future

| Form | Use | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| **Present Simple** | Scheduled events, timetables | The train **leaves** at 4. |
| **Present Continuous** | Plans or arrangements | I**'m playing** football tomorrow. |
| **Be Going To** | Plans or intentions | I**'m going to drive** to work today. |
| **Be Going To** | Predictions based on evidence | Look at those clouds! It**'s going to rain**. |
| **Will be + -ing** | Plans, arrangements, intentions | They**'ll be coming** to see us next week. |
| **Modals (may, might, could)** | Uncertainty about the future | I **might stay** at home tonight. |
| **Should** | Good chance of something happening | We **should be** home in time for tea. |

## Summary Table: All Future Forms

| Form | Structure | Example | Main Use |
|------|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Simple Future** | will + V | I will go. | General future, instant decisions, predictions |
| **Future Progressive** | will be + V-ing | I will be going. | Action in progress at a future time |
| **Future Perfect** | will have + V3 | I will have gone. | Action completed before a future time |
| **Future Perfect Progressive** | will have been + V-ing | I will have been going. | Duration of action up to a future time |
| **Be Going To** | am/is/are + going to + V | I am going to go. | Plans, intentions, evidence-based predictions |
| **Present Continuous** | am/is/are + V-ing | I am going. | Arrangements, definite plans |
| **Present Simple** | base verb (+s) | The train leaves at 4. | Timetables, schedules |

## Key Points to Remember

1. **Future Simple** (will + V) — general future, instant decisions, predictions.
2. **Future Progressive** (will be + V-ing) — action in progress at a future time.
3. **Future Perfect** (will have + V3) — action completed before a future time.
4. **Future Perfect Progressive** (will have been + V-ing) — duration up to a future time.
5. **Be Going To** — plans, intentions, evidence-based predictions.
6. **Present Continuous** — arrangements, definite plans.
7. **Present Simple** — timetables, schedules.
8. Use **shall** with I/we for offers, suggestions, and formal future.
9. **Stative verbs** cannot be used in continuous forms.
10. Use **may, might, could** for uncertainty about the future.
