### Positive Sentences

**Subject + verb to be + complement**

- I am a student.
- He is my friend.
- She is at home.
- We are brothers.
- You are a good student.
- Tatiana is a good driver.
- They are classmates.

### Negative Sentences

- You aren't a doctor.
- He is not my brother.
- We are not good friends.
- She isn't my professor.
- They're not here.

### Wh- Questions Structure
