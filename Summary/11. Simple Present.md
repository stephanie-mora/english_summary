
# Simple Present

## Introduction

The **Simple Present** is one of the most important tenses in English. We use it to talk about:

- **Habits** and routines
- **General truths** and facts
- **Permanent situations**
- **Feelings, thoughts, and states**

### Positive Sentences

> **Structure:** **Subject + verb (+ -s/-es for he/she/it) + complement**

| Subject | Verb | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| I | drink | I **drink** coffee every morning. |
| You | drink | You **drink** coffee every morning. |
| He | drinks | He **drinks** coffee every morning. |
| She | drinks | She **drinks** coffee every morning. |
| It | drinks | It **drinks** milk. |
| We | drink | We **drink** coffee every morning. |
| You | drink | You **drink** coffee every morning. |
| They | drink | They **drink** coffee every morning. |

### Negative Sentences

> **Structure:** **Subject + do/does + not + verb + complement**

| Subject | Auxiliary | Example |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| I / You / We / They | **do not (don't)** | I **don't drink** coffee. |
| He / She / It | **does not (doesn't)** | She **doesn't drink** coffee. |

> **Important:** The verb after **don't/doesn't** is always in the **base form** (no -s, -es, -ies).

**More Examples**

- You **don't speak** Arabic.
- John **doesn't speak** Italian.
- We **don't have** time for a rest.
- It **doesn't move**.
- They **don't want** to go to the party.
- She **doesn't like** fish.

### Questions

> **Structure:** **Do/Does + subject + verb + complement?**

| Auxiliary | Subject | Example |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| **Do** | I / you / we / they | **Do** you **drink** coffee? |
| **Does** | he / she / it | **Does** she **drink** coffee? |

> **Important:** The verb after **do/does** is always in the **base form**.

**More Examples**

- **Do** you **need** a dictionary?
- **Does** Mary **need** a dictionary?
- **Do** we **have** a meeting now?
- **Does** it **rain** a lot in winter?
- **Do** they **want** to go to the party?
- **Does** he **like** pizza?

### Short Answers

| Affirmative | Negative |
|-------------|----------|
| Yes, I / you / we / they **do**. | No, I / you / we / they **don't**. |
| Yes, he / she / it **does**. | No, he / she / it **doesn't**. |

**Examples:**

| Question | Short Answer |
|----------|--------------|
| Do you like coffee? | Yes, I **do**. / No, I **don't**. |
| Does she speak English? | Yes, she **does**. / No, she **doesn't**. |
| Do they live here? | Yes, they **do**. / No, they **don't**. |
| Does he work here? | Yes, he **does**. / No, he **doesn't**. |

## Third Person Singular (Tercera Persona del Singular)

For **he, she, it**, we add **-s** or **-es** to the verb.

### Spelling Rules

| Rule | When to Use | Examples |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Add -s** | Most verbs | drink → drink**s**, run → run**s**, play → play**s** |
| **Add -es** | Verbs ending in **-ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -o, -z** | watch → watch**es**, go → go**es**, kiss → kiss**es**, wash → wash**es**, fix → fix**es**, buzz → buzz**es** |
| **Change -y to -ies** | Consonant + y | cry → cr**ies**, study → stud**ies**, fly → fl**ies** |
| **Add -s** | Vowel + y | play → play**s**, enjoy → enjoy**s**, buy → buy**s** |

**Examples**

| Infinitive | He / She / It |
|------------|---------------|
| I drink | he **drinks** |
| I run | he **runs** |
| I watch | he **watches** |
| I go | he **goes** |
| I kiss | she **kisses** |
| I wash | she **washes** |
| I fix | it **fixes** |
| I cry | he **cries** |
| I study | she **studies** |
| I play | he **plays** |
| I enjoy | she **enjoys** |

## Use of the Simple Present

### 1. Habits and regular actions

Actions that we do regularly or repeatedly.

- He **visits** his friends every Sunday.
- She **goes** to school by bus.
- I **drink** coffee every morning.
- They **play** tennis on Saturdays.
- We **eat** dinner at 7 PM.

**Time expressions:** every day, every week, always, usually, often, sometimes, never.

### 2. General truths

Facts that are always true.

- The sun **rises** in the East.
- Water **boils** at 100 degrees Celsius.
- Cats **like** milk.
- The Earth **goes** around the Sun.
- Plants **need** water to grow.

### 3. Permanent situations

Situations that are long-term or permanent.

- He **lives** in Athens.
- She **works** as a doctor.
- They **speak** French.
- I **have** two brothers.
- We **are** from Mexico.

### 4. Feelings, Thoughts, and States

Verbs that describe feelings, thoughts, or states are usually used in the Simple Present.

- I **love** chocolate.
- She **wants** a new phone.
- He **knows** the answer.
- They **believe** in ghosts.
- We **understand** the problem.

**Common stative verbs:** love, like, hate, want, need, know, believe, understand, remember, forget, seem, appear, cost, weigh, own, belong.

### 5. Timetables and Schedules

We use the Simple Present for scheduled events in the future.

- The train **leaves** at 8 AM tomorrow.
- The movie **starts** at 7 PM.
- The plane **arrives** at 3 PM.
- School **begins** on Monday.

### 6. Detailed Uses

| Use | Example |
|-----|---------|
| **Repeated or regular actions** | I **take** the train to the office. |
| **Facts** | The President of the USA **lives** in The White House. |
| **Habits** | I **get up** early every day. |
| **Things that are always/generally true** | It **rains** a lot in winter. |

## Important Exception: To Be and Modal Verbs

> **Important note:** We do **NOT** use **Do/Does** with the verb **To Be** or with **Modal Verbs** (can, must, might, should, etc.).

| Verb Type | ❌ Incorrect | ✅ Correct |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| **To Be** | *Do you be happy?* | *Are you happy?* |
| **Modal Verbs** | *Does she can swim?* | *Can she swim?* |
| **Modal Verbs** | *Do they must go?* | *Must they go?* |

**Examples:**
- **Are** you happy? (not *Do you be happy?*)
- **Can** she swim? (not *Does she can swim?*)
- **Must** they go? (not *Do they must go?*)


## Structure Summary

| Type | Structure | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Positive** | Subject + verb (+ -s/-es) + complement | She **drinks** coffee. |
| **Negative** | Subject + do/does + not + verb + complement | She **doesn't drink** coffee. |
| **Question** | Do/Does + subject + verb + complement? | **Does** she **drink** coffee? |
| **Short Answer** | Yes/No + subject + do/does | Yes, she **does**. |

## Key Points to Remember

1. Use **-s** or **-es** for **he, she, it**.
2. Use **do** with **I, you, we, they**.
3. Use **does** with **he, she, it**.
4. Use the **base verb** after **do/does**.
5. Use the Simple Present for **habits**, **general truths**, **permanent situations**, **feelings**, and **timetables**.
6. **Stative verbs** (love, want, know, etc.) are usually used in the Simple Present.
7. **Do NOT use Do/Does** with **To Be** or **Modal Verbs**.
8. The verb after **don't/doesn't** is always in the **base form**.
