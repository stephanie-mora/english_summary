
# Future - Present Progressive

## Introduction

The **present progressive** can be used to express **future time**, especially for **definite plans** made before the moment of speaking.

---

## Uses

### 1. Definite Plans for the Future

The present progressive describes **definite plans** for the future — plans that were made **before** the moment of speaking.

**Examples:**

| Be Going To | Present Progressive |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Tom **is going to come** to the party tomorrow. | Tom **is coming** to the party tomorrow. |
| We**'re going to go** to a movie tonight. | We**'re going** to a movie tonight. |
| I**'m going to stay** home this evening. | I**'m staying** home this evening. |
| Ann **is going to fly** to Chicago next week. | Ann **is flying** to Chicago next week. |

> **Note:** Each pair has the **same meaning**.

### 2. Future Meaning Indicated by Time Words

A future meaning for the present progressive is indicated by **future time words** (tomorrow, tonight, next week) or by the situation.

### 3. NOT Used for Predictions

The present progressive is **NOT** used for predictions about the future.

- ✅ *You**'re going to laugh** when you hear this joke.* (prediction)
- ❌ *You**'re laughing** when you hear this joke.* (incorrect — laughing is a prediction, not a planned event)

---

## Summary Table

| Use | Present Progressive |
|-----|---------------------|
| **Definite plans** | ✅ |
| **Predictions** | ❌ |

---

## Key Points

1. Use the **present progressive** for definite future plans.
2. Plans must be made **before** the moment of speaking.
3. Future meaning is shown by **time words** (tomorrow, tonight, next week).
4. **Do NOT** use the present progressive for predictions.
5. The present progressive and **be going to** are often interchangeable for plans.
