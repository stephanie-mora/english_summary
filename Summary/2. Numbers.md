**Note:** In the written versions, we add st, nd or th to the number.

**Example:**
- first -> 1st
- second -> 2nd
- tenth -> 10th

### Dates

We use numeral adjectives to express dates.

**Examples:**
- **10th April, 2008**
  - UK: the tenth of April, two thousand and eight
  - US: April (the) tenth, two thousand and eight / April 10, 2008 / April ten(th), two thousand eight

### Centuries and Titles

We also express centuries and titles (of Kings, Queens, Popes, etc.) with numeral adjectives:

**Example:**
- Charles I (1600-1649)
	  - "Did you know that Charles the First lived in the seventeenth century?"
	  - "Really? You're so clever! The 21st century is a lot more interesting with you in it."
