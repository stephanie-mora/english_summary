
## Greetings (Saludos)

Greetings are used to say hello and start a conversation. Different greetings are used in different situations (formal/informal).

| Greeting | Use |
|----------|-----|
| **Hi! / Hello!** | The most common and universal greetings. Use with anyone. |
| **Good morning** | Before 12:00 PM (noon). |
| **Good afternoon** | From 12:00 PM to 6:00 PM. |
| **Good evening** | After 6:00 PM. |
| **How are you?** | A common way to ask about someone's well-being. |
| **How have you been?** | Used when you haven't seen someone for a while. |
| **How is everything?** | Asks about someone's life in general. |
| **How is it going?** | Casual and friendly way to ask how someone is. |
| **How are you doing?** | Similar to "How are you?" but more casual. |
| **What's up?** | Very informal greeting. Use with friends. |

**Examples**

- **Hi!** How are you?
- **Good morning**, everyone!
- **How is everything** going?

## Responses to Greetings (Respuestas)

These are common answers to "How are you?" and similar questions.

| Response | Meaning/Use |
|----------|-------------|
| **I'm fine, thanks, and you?** | Standard polite response. |
| **Not bad, and you?** | Means "I'm okay" - casual and friendly. |
| **I'm very good, thank you.** | More formal and positive. |
| **Good, how about you?** | Brief and friendly. |
| **Great, how about you?** | Very positive response. |
| **I'm ok, how about you?** | Neutral response. |

**Examples**

> - **A:** How are you?
> - **B:** I'm fine, thanks, and you?

> - **A:** How is it going?
> - **B:** Not bad, and you?

## Saying Goodbye

These are common ways to say goodbye in English. Some are more formal, others more casual.

| Goodbye | Use |
|---------|-----|
| **Bye-bye!** | Casual, often used with children. |
| **Good bye** | Standard and polite. |
| **See you soon** | When you expect to see the person soon. |
| **See you later** | Very common and casual. |
| **See you!** | Short and casual. |
| **So long!** | Old-fashioned but still used. |
| **Take care!** | Friendly and caring. |
| **Good night** | When saying goodbye at night. |
| **I got to go** | Casual way to say you need to leave. |
| **I have to go** | Polite way to say you need to leave. |
| **I must go** | More direct, shows urgency. |

**Examples**

- **Bye-bye!** See you later!
- **Good night**, sleep well!
- **I have to go**, it was nice talking to you.


## Introducing People (Presentaciones)

When you want to introduce someone, use these phrases.

### Phrases for Introducing

| Phrase | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **This is + [name]** | Introduce one person. |
| **These are + [name(s)]** | Introduce two or more people. |
| **They are + [name(s)]** | Introduce others. |
| **He/She is my + [relation]** | Explain the relationship. |
 
**Examples**

- **This is** Bertha.
- **These are** my friends.
- **They are** Ruth and Jeremy.
- **He is** my brother.
- **She is** my sister.
- **This is** my mother.

## Responses to Introductions (Respuestas a Presentaciones)

After being introduced to someone, you should respond politely.

| Response | Meaning/Use |
|----------|-------------|
| **Nice to meet you.** | Standard and polite. |
| **Pleased to meet you.** | More formal. |
| **Nice meeting you.** | Used when saying goodbye after first meeting. |
| **It's a pleasure.** | Formal and polite. |
| **Nice to meet you too.** | Return the greeting. |
| **Pleased to meet you too.** | Formal response. |
| **Nice meeting you too.** | Response to "Nice meeting you." |
| **Likewise.** | Means "Same here" or "Me too." |

**Examples**

> - **A:** This is my friend, Maria.
> - **B:** Hi! Nice to meet you.
> - **C:** Nice to meet you too.

> - **A:** These are my parents.
> - **B:** Pleased to meet you.
> - **C:** Likewise.

## Dialog Example (Ejemplo de Diálogo)

> - **A:** Hello! How are you?
> - **B:** I'm great, thanks! And you?
> - **A:** Not bad! By the way, this is my friend, John.
> - **B:** Nice to meet you, John.
> - **C:** Nice to meet you too.
> - **B:** Well, I have to go. See you later!
> - **A & C:** Bye! Take care!
