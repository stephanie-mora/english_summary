
## Days of the Week (Días de la Semana)

The week has 7 days. In English, days of the week **always** start with a capital letter.

| Day       | Abbreviation |
|-----------|--------------|
| Monday    | Mon.         |
| Tuesday   | Tue. / Tues. |
| Wednesday | Wed.         |
| Thursday  | Thu. / Thurs.|
| Friday    | Fri.         |
| Saturday  | Sat.         |
| Sunday    | Sun.         |

### Prepositions with Days

| Use | Example |
|-----|---------|
| **On** + day | *I have class **on Monday**.* |
| **Every** + day | *I go to the gym **every Tuesday**.* |
| **From... to...** | *The store is open **from Monday to Friday**.* |
| **On** + day + morning/afternoon/evening | *We will meet **on Monday morning**.* |

> **Important:** Use **on** with specific days. Example: *on Friday*, *on Sunday*.

## Months of the Year (Meses del Año)

There are 12 months in a year. In English, months **always** start with a capital letter.

| Month     | Abbreviation |
|-----------|--------------|
| January   | Jan.         |
| February  | Feb.         |
| March     | Mar.         |
| April     | Apr.         |
| May       | May          |
| June      | Jun.         |
| July      | Jul.         |
| August    | Aug.         |
| September | Sep. / Sept. |
| October   | Oct.         |
| November  | Nov.         |
| December  | Dec.         |

### Prepositions with Months

| Use | Example |
|-----|---------|
| **In** + month | *My birthday is **in July**.* |
| **In** + month + year | *I moved here **in March 2020**.* |
| **Every** + month | *We go on vacation **every August**.* |

> **Important:** Use **in** with months. Example: *in January*, *in December*.

## Seasons (Estaciones)

There are 4 seasons in a year. In English, seasons **do not** always start with a capital letter (unless at the beginning of a sentence).

| Season        | Months                       |
|---------------|------------------------------|
| Spring        | March, April, May            |
| Summer        | June, July, August           |
| Autumn / Fall | September, October, November |
| Winter        | December, January, February  |

### Prepositions with Seasons

| Use | Example |
|-----|---------|
| **In** + season | *We go to the beach **in summer**.* |
| **During** + season | *It rains a lot **during spring**.* |
| **In the** + season | *The leaves fall **in the autumn**.* |

> **Note:** 
> - **Autumn** is used in British English.
> - **Fall** is used in American English.
> - Both are correct and understood in all English-speaking countries.

## Important Rules

### 1. Capitalization (Mayúsculas)
- **Always** capitalize days and months: *Monday, January*
- **Not always** capitalize seasons: *spring, summer, autumn, fall*

### 2. Prepositions (Preposiciones)

| Preposition | Use | Example |
|-------------|-----|---------|
| **On** | Days of the week | *on Monday* |
| **In** | Months | *in July* |
| **In** | Seasons | *in winter* |
| **In** | Years | *in 2020* |
| **At** | Specific times | *at 3 o'clock* |
| **On** | Dates | *on July 4th* |

### 3. Word Order in Dates

| Format | Example |
|--------|---------|
| **UK** (Day-Month-Year) | *10th April, 2008* |
| **US** (Month-Day-Year) | *April 10, 2008* |


## Common Expressions

### Days

| Expression | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **Weekdays** | Monday to Friday |
| **Weekend** | Saturday and Sunday |
| **Next week** | The week after this one |
| **Last week** | The week before this one |
| **Every day** | Each day |

### Months

| Expression | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **This month** | The current month |
| **Next month** | The month after this one |
| **Last month** | The month before this one |
| **Every month** | Each month |


**Examples in Context**

### Days

- I work **on Monday**.
- She goes to the gym **every Tuesday**.
- We are free **this weekend**.
- The office is closed **on Saturdays**.

### Months

- My birthday is **in May**.
- Christmas is **in December**.
- We are going on holiday **in August**.
- She started her new job **in March**.

### Seasons

- It is very hot **in summer**.
- Leaves change color **in autumn**.
- We go skiing **in winter**.
- Flowers bloom **in spring**.

## Full Date Format Examples
| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| **UK** | *Monday, 10th April 2008* |
| **US** | *Monday, April 10th, 2008* |
| **UK (written)** | *Monday, the tenth of April, two thousand and eight* |
| **US (written)** | *Monday, April tenth, two thousand and eight* |

## Dialog Example

> **A:** What day is it today?
> **B:** It's **Tuesday**.
> **A:** Great! What month is it?
> **B:** It's **October**.
> **A:** Oh, so it's **autumn**! I love this season.
> **B:** Me too! The weather is perfect.

## Key Points to Remember

1. Days and months **always** start with a capital letter.
2. Seasons **do not** always start with a capital letter.
3. Use **on** + day → *on Monday*
4. Use **in** + month → *in January*
5. Use **in** + season → *in summer*
6. Use **in** + year → *in 2020*
7. Use **on** + date → *on July 4th*
