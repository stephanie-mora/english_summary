# Prepositions

## Prepositions of Place (Lugar)

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| on | sobre | The book is **on** the table. |
| upon | sobre (formal) | Once **upon** a time... |
| at | en | She is **at** home. |
| inside | dentro de | The cat is **inside** the box. |
| outside | fuera de | The dog is **outside** the house. |
| above | encima de (sin tocar) | The lamp is **above** the table. |
| below | debajo de | The shoes are **below** the bed. |
| along | a lo largo de | We walked **along** the beach. |
| around | alrededor de | They sat **around** the fire. |
| against | contra | He leaned **against** the wall. |
| on top of | encima de | The book is **on top of** the shelf. |
| at the bottom of | al fondo de | The boat is **at the bottom of** the lake. |
| in front of | delante de | The car is **in front of** the house. |

## Prepositions of Direction (Dirección)

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| down | abajo | She walked **down** the stairs. |
| into | hacia dentro | He went **into** the room. |
| out of | fuera de | She ran **out of** the house. |
| on | sobre (movimiento) | He jumped **on** the table. |
| off | fuera de | The cat jumped **off** the table. |
| over | por encima | The bird flew **over** the house. |
| under | debajo | The dog crawled **under** the bed. |
| in front of | delante de | He walked **in front of** me. |

## Prepositions of Time (Tiempo)

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| since | desde | I've lived here **since** 2010. |
| until | hasta | Wait **until** 5 PM. |
| to | a (hora) | It's a quarter **to** five. |
| past | pasada (hora) | It's half **past** three. |
| between | entre | The store is open **between** 9 and 5. |
| at | a (hora exacta) | The class starts **at** 8 o'clock. |
| on | en (día) | We meet **on** Wednesday. |
| in | en (mes/año) | My birthday is **in** May. |

---

## Key Points

1. **Place:** on, in, at, inside, outside, above, below, along, around.
2. **Direction:** down, into, out of, over, under, off.
3. **Time:** at (hour), on (day), in (month/year), since, until, between.
4. Prepositions are often followed by **nouns** or **pronouns**.
