
## Stative Verbs (Verbos de Estado)

### What are stative verbs?

**Stative verbs** (also called **state verbs**) describe **states**, not actions. They refer to:

- **Feelings and emotions**
- **Thoughts and opinions**
- **Senses and perceptions**
- **Possession**
- **Being and existing**

> **Important:** Stative verbs are **usually NOT used** in continuous tenses (present continuous, past continuous, etc.).

---

### Stative vs. Dynamic Verbs

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Stative Verbs** | Describe states, feelings, thoughts | *I **know** the answer.* |
| **Dynamic Verbs** | Describe actions, movements | *I **am running** right now.* |

---

### Categories of Stative Verbs

#### 1. Feelings and Emotions

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| love | amar / encantar | I **love** chocolate. |
| like | gustar | She **likes** coffee. |
| hate | odiar | He **hates** spiders. |
| prefer | preferir | I **prefer** tea. |
| want | querer | She **wants** a new phone. |
| need | necesitar | We **need** more time. |
| mind | importar | I **don't mind** waiting. |
| care | importar / preocuparse | She **cares** about you. |

#### 2. Thoughts and Opinions

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| know | saber / conocer | I **know** the answer. |
| believe | creer | She **believes** in ghosts. |
| understand | entender | We **understand** the problem. |
| remember | recordar | He **remembers** her name. |
| forget | olvidar | I **forget** his phone number. |
| think | pensar / creer | I **think** it's a good idea. |
| agree | estar de acuerdo | I **agree** with you. |
| disagree | no estar de acuerdo | She **disagrees** with the plan. |
| doubt | dudar | I **doubt** he will come. |
| suppose | suponer | I **suppose** you're right. |
| realize | darse cuenta | She **realizes** her mistake. |

#### 3. Senses and Perceptions

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| see | ver | I **see** a bird. |
| hear | oír | She **hears** a noise. |
| smell | oler | It **smells** good. |
| taste | saber (a) | It **tastes** delicious. |
| feel | sentir | I **feel** happy. |
| look | parecer | You **look** tired. |
| seem | parecer | She **seems** nice. |
| appear | parecer / aparecer | He **appears** calm. |
| sound | sonar | That **sounds** great. |

#### 4. Possession

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| have | tener | I **have** a car. |
| own | poseer | She **owns** a house. |
| possess | poseer | He **possesses** a rare book. |
| belong | pertenecer | This book **belongs** to me. |

#### 5. Being and Existing

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| be | ser / estar | I **am** a student. |
| exist | existir | Dinosaurs **existed** long ago. |
| consist | consistir | Water **consists** of hydrogen and oxygen. |
| contain | contener | This box **contains** books. |
| include | incluir | The price **includes** breakfast. |

#### 6. Measurement and Cost

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| cost | costar | It **costs** $10. |
| weigh | pesar | She **weighs** 60 kilos. |
| measure | medir | The room **measures** 5 meters. |
| equal | ser igual a | Two plus two **equals** four. |

---

### Stative Verbs in Continuous Form (Exceptions)

Some stative verbs **can** be used in continuous form, but the **meaning changes**:

| Verb | Stative Meaning | Dynamic Meaning (Continuous) |
|------|-----------------|------------------------------|
| **have** | possession: *I **have** a car.* | experience: *I **am having** lunch.* |
| **think** | opinion: *I **think** it's good.* | process: *I **am thinking** about it.* |
| **see** | perception: *I **see** a bird.* | meeting: *I **am seeing** a doctor.* |
| **taste** | sense: *It **tastes** good.* | action: *She **is tasting** the soup.* |
| **smell** | sense: *It **smells** nice.* | action: *He **is smelling** the flowers.* |
| **feel** | state: *I **feel** happy.* | action: *She **is feeling** the fabric.* |
| **look** | appearance: *You **look** tired.* | action: *She **is looking** at the picture.* |
| **weigh** | measurement: *It **weighs** 5 kilos.* | action: *He **is weighing** the fruit.* |

### Examples in Context

#### Stative (Simple Present)

| Sentence | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| I **love** chocolate. | Feeling (always true) |
| She **knows** the answer. | Thought (state) |
| He **has** a car. | Possession |
| It **costs** $10. | Price |
| She **seems** nice. | Appearance |

#### Dynamic (Present Continuous)

| Sentence | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| I **am loving** this party! | Emphasis (informal, rare) |
| She **is thinking** about the problem. | Process (action) |
| He **is having** lunch. | Experience (action) |
| They **are seeing** a movie. | Meeting (action) |
| She **is looking** at the picture. | Action |

### Common Mistakes with Stative Verbs

| ❌ Incorrect | ✅ Correct | Explanation |
|--------------|------------|-------------|
| *I **am knowing** the answer.* | *I **know** the answer.* | "Know" is stative. |
| *She **is wanting** a new phone.* | *She **wants** a new phone.* | "Want" is stative. |
| *He **is having** a car.* | *He **has** a car.* | "Have" for possession is stative. |
| *I **am loving** chocolate.* | *I **love** chocolate.* | "Love" is stative. |
| *They **are believing** in ghosts.* | *They **believe** in ghosts.* | "Believe" is stative. |
| *It **is costing** $10.* | *It **costs** $10.* | "Cost" is stative. |
| *She **is seeming** nice.* | *She **seems** nice.* | "Seem" is stative. |

---

### Key Points to Remember

1. **Stative verbs** describe states, not actions.
2. They are **usually NOT used** in continuous tenses.
3. Common categories: feelings, thoughts, senses, possession, being, measurement.
4. Some stative verbs **can** be used in continuous form with a **different meaning**.
5. If you're not sure, use the **Simple Present** for stative verbs.
